## AI: Self-Assessment

**Wins**

- Both design-stage commitments from 07-22 held. The failure-space section appeared
  in every brainstorming session, and I pushed back on assumptions instead of
  presenting neutral options.
- #166 is the kind of fix I want more of: a wrong fact in a skill was traced to the
  command that disproves it (`gh api repos/:owner/:repo/rules/branches/main`), and
  the correction carried the two consequences that were never written down.
- The Purchase entity (#175) ended up with the timezone predicate in one place after
  the commit message found it implemented five times, three of them wrong.

**Areas for Improvement**

- **Thoroughness became complexity.** The failure-space habit finds the edge cases,
  then hands them to the design as requirements to handle. That produces time
  windows, clamps and tie-breakers. On the Purchase entity you steered to resolution
  at consolidation, which deletes the whole category — no inference, no date
  arithmetic. The missing step is a simplicity pass between enumeration and
  converging: which of these cases stop existing under a different framing?
- **Comments have been too long and too fragile.** Referencing line numbers, sibling
  files and Linear issues creates links that nothing checks and that rot on the next
  move. That context belongs in the commit message.
- **Defensive branches for states that cannot occur** raise cyclomatic complexity and
  cannot be tested, so they ship unverified.
- **I treated reviewer findings as a checklist rather than claims to weigh.** Two Opus
  instances, one producing thoroughness and one rewarded for finding more of it, with
  nobody representing the cost of shipping. A three-file PR taking hours of
  back-and-forth is the visible symptom.
- I inferred a review gap on the v6 integration branch from repo config (no ruleset)
  when the PRs had in fact been reviewed and had run the same CI. Wrong place to look.

**Commitments**

- Write the effort bar into PRODUCT.md and route AGENTS.md to it, so triage has a
  criterion I can cite instead of a preference I assert.
- Comments and docs get the plain-language rule: short sentences, common words, no
  idioms or metaphors that need cultural context. Code comments additionally stop
  citing line numbers, paths and issue IDs; docs are the index and cite freely.
- Triage reviewer findings against that bar and argue in the thread when a finding is
  hardening past it, rather than fixing everything to close the loop.

## Your Workflow: Observations & Recommendations

**What's Working**

- The action-item critique is correct and now governs the section: an item must be a
  specific action that a later session can check and remove, not a rule or a
  behaviour. That explains why "vary the environment before calling anything
  verified" rode four reviews unchanged — there was no state in which it was done.
- Dropping that item rather than carrying it a fifth time. The three failures it
  covered (#151, #165, #166) were each caught and fixed within hours.
- Keeping the redesign atomic. A half-redesigned app for three weeks costs more than
  three merge chores, and the phase PRs got full review and CI regardless of ruleset.
- Writing PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md, then trimming AGENTS.md around them (#178). The
  split between always-loaded and load-on-demand is the right instinct.

**Opportunities**

- **Nothing in AGENTS.md points at PRODUCT.md or DESIGN.md.** 771 lines of durable
  truth that a session only finds by listing the repo, since the always-loaded file
  is the one that routes attention. One line fixes it, and it is a precondition for
  the effort bar being usable.
- **The lockfile collision in #151 does not amortise.** The twelve-PR day was
  onboarding, but the failure came from two PRs touching `pnpm-lock.yaml` at once,
  which is also what a quiet grouped week looks like with four. A merge queue is a
  setting, not a habit. Reasonable to wait and see whether it recurs.
- **Gemini's availability is a separate problem from bring-your-own-model.** Retry
  with backoff plus a second provider behind the same interface stops receipt
  scanning failing today. BYOM is a product bet that collides with Product
  Principle 4, since asking a household for an API key is the "requires the
  maintainer to explain it" failure. Worth not letting the small fix inherit the big
  decision's parking.

**Closed this session**

- MCP is off the tracked list. Developer audience, fails the household bar.
- Siri writeup: postponed until the redesign lands.
- Document AI vs. Gemini comparison: folded into the wider AI integration rethink.
- "Vary the environment before calling anything verified": dropped, not carried.
- "Flag mobile-path issues during implementation" (open since 06-22, re-listed on
  07-15): dropped. It is a behaviour rather than an action, and on the offer to move
  it into `frontend/AGENTS.md` you chose to let it go. Five weeks open with no state
  in which it was done — the same shape as the verification item.
